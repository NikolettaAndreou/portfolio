Thesis & Research

Secure IoMT Medication Monitoring System Completed

Bachelor's thesis project focused on securing Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) environments by combining machine learning, cybersecurity mechanisms, healthcare data analysis, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI).

The system analyzes medication infusion records from the MIMIC-IV clinical database, detects anomalous behaviour caused by simulated cyberattacks or unsafe device operation, and presents the results through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

  • Processed and analyzed 1+ million clinical medication records.
  • Simulated rate manipulation, sudden rate change, time-gap manipulation, and replay attacks.
  • Evaluated Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, and Autoencoder models.
  • Achieved 95.75% accuracy and 97.9% anomaly recall with the best-performing Random Forest model.
  • Integrated AES encryption, HMAC-SHA256 integrity verification, replay attack detection, and XAI explanations.
  • Built a dashboard with live data-stream simulation, anomaly alerts, security monitoring, feature importance, and rule-based explanations.

Technologies: Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Streamlit · Cryptography · Machine Learning · Cybersecurity · Explainable AI

Role: Sole Developer · Bachelor Thesis Project · 2026
